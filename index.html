WEBSTRIKE

Tactical 5v5 bomb defusal, right in your browser

Online (peer-to-peer, works on GitHub Pages)

Dedicated server on a PC

Controls & settings

WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB scope (sniper) · Space jump · Shift walk · C crouch
Q / E lean left / right (your hitbox leans too, and other players see it)
R reload · 1/2/3 primary/secondary/knife · B operator select (prep phase) · Tab scoreboard
G or Mouse 5 use your operator's gadget (hold it for Ghost Walk) · browser back/forward are blocked during a match
Hold F on a bomb site to plant (Attack) or next to the bomb to defuse (Defense) · during prep, F places / removes a barricade on the doorway you face (Defense)
Shoot or knife walls and barricades to break through. Squads swap sides every 3 rounds; first squad to 8 round wins takes the match.
